# Sensei Desk: what we learned, 26-28 Sep

Follows [improvements-2026-09-25.md](improvements-2026-09-25.md). Each finding comes from a live
session log, a replay of recorded audio, the teaching benchmark, or a model comparison run on the
Spark; the evidence is named with each.

## 1. Teaching: Sensei spots mistakes but doesn't yet teach

**Benchmark** (`gateway/evals/teaching/`): 24 synthetic students on 10 real sample problems (math,
physics, chemistry; personas stuck, confidently wrong, wants the why, terse and noisy), talking to
the real tutor (prompts, Jev, `qwen3-vl-30b`, the actual page image), one turn at a time. Students
and blind judges were played by Claude. 48 conversations.

| | current | teach mode |
|---|---|---|
| overall (out of 10) | 4.0 | 3.7 |
| students who reached the fix | 18/24 | 18/24 |
| answered what the student said (0-2) | 0.88 | 0.50 |
| conversations that looped | 7 | 15 |

- Teach mode (explain in 2-4 spoken sentences with a small different example, then hand it back)
  produced some of the best moments, but it repeated the same explanation, so loops doubled. It
  stays off (`SENSEI_TEACH_MODE`).
- The failures are missing **state**, not wording:
  1. Sensei doesn't notice a fix said out loud; the mistake is only cleared by a page re-read.
  2. It doesn't know the right answer; it approved "O2 limiting, 72 g" and "x = -2" and called
     correct work wrong.
  3. It repeats a hint or explanation instead of changing approach.
  4. "Why" questions got the rule again, not a reason.
  5. A mistake on a late line is blamed on line 1 (all four m4 conversations).
- **Next:** an answer key (the careful model solves once, privately; sympy checks the maths), a
  lesson state (hinted, explained, attempted, fixed), and no repeats. Re-run the same 24 students.

## 2. Reading the page

- **New sheet, old problem:** every look said "the problem in focus is X, judge only X", so a new
  sheet was forced into the old problem with invented steps. Fix: the earlier problem is
  background ("read what is on the page now"), and focus moves when the old problem is gone from
  the page. Checked on the real model with a new sample page.
- **"8KEd" for "8K Edu":** the background looks read it right. Replies repeated the misreading
  because Jev filed the student's corrections as "subject", whose rule forbids looking at the
  camera. Fix: a correction keeps the image, overrides Jev's label, and asks for a letter-by-letter
  re-read. Replayed on the real frame: "You're right, it says 8K Edu" (4/4).
- The fast vision model's weaknesses stand: it flags 6 of 7 correct pages and blames line 1 for
  late mistakes; `qwen3.8-27b` had 0/7 false alarms at 23 s per page.

## 3. Listening

- **A Whisper prompt is dangerous in noise.** A tutoring prompt ending "x plus 5 equals 7, so x
  equals 2" was echoed back as "x plus 5 equals 6." twelve times in one noisy session, and Sensei
  answered the echoes. Removed; no prompt.
- **Bigger Whisper doesn't help.** On the same session audio, small.en, medium.en and
  large-v3-turbo all misheard "Edu" (and the bigger two were 3-5x slower and invented more).
- **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2** (ONNX, CPU; near the top of the Open ASR leaderboard at 0.6 B) matched
  Whisper's accuracy on that audio, was 6x faster (0.17 s vs 1.1 s per utterance) and stayed silent
  on noise. It is the default now; swap live with `POST /ears {"engine": "whisper"}`.
- The limit is the audio (a phone mic across a noisy desk), not the model. Push-to-talk in the app
  or a clip-on mic would help more than any model.

## 4. Fast decisions (Jev)

Full results in [jev-report.md](jev-report.md) §9. Of JevK5, Imajev-4B, Plumb-4B, Hopper and
CLM-v0.1-8B, **Imajev-4B** (1 rotation, `--fast --merge-lora`) is best on every decision at
JevK5's latency (373 ms): should reply 43/47, about 30/37 (JevK5 25/37), needs the page 17/25,
new problem 44/44, and nearly always right when confident. CLM-8B is 13 ms but unusable for typed
decisions (it answered every non-question). Under four simultaneous synthetic students, JevK5
timed out on 12% of calls at the 1.5 s limit; Sensei decided without it each time, as designed.

## 5. Connectivity and operations

- **Calls dropping after the first question:** every call ended as "closed", never "failed". The
  phone was on the venue Wi-Fi and the Spark on a phone hotspot, so all media went phone ->
  internet -> Funnel -> coturn over one TCP connection; coturn logged "TCP socket error: Connection
  reset by peer". Put the phone and the Spark on the same network (media then goes direct), or use
  `http://<spark LAN IP>:8787`.
- **SenseiDesk picture freezing:** full 1280x720 at 30 fps over a hotspot uplink. Desks now get
  640 px at 12 fps (`SENSEI_DESK_LONG_SIDE`, `SENSEI_DESK_FPS`), and the page reconnects a frozen
  picture by itself (after 3 tries, the MJPEG preview).
- **Every restart or move takes everything down** (three times in two days). After a move the TURN
  relay IP must follow the LAN IP. `/root/.claude/skills/sensei-desk-connect/scripts/diagnose.sh`
  checks all of it; start-on-boot services are still to do.
- **One model for the box:** the gateway and Sensei-NemoClaw (the web app) both use
  `qwen3-vl-30b-a3b-gguf`, so the router never swaps between them.

## 6. What's next, in order
