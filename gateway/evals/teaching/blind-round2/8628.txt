PROBLEM: A 5.0 kg block slides down a 30 degree incline, friction coefficient 0.20. Find the friction force. g = 9.8
THE REAL MISTAKE ON THE PAGE: Line 1 wrote N = mg = 49 N: on a slope the normal force is mg cos 30 = 42.4 N. Friction should be 8.5 N; student got 9.8 N.
STUDENT PERSONA: wants-why - Accepts mg cos 30 after a hint, then asks why it's cos and not sin.
